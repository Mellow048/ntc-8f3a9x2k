# Third-party 3D assets

The following CC BY vehicle models are used as bridge traffic, scaled, rotated and lit in the scene. Their source repository records them as Creative Commons Attribution:

- **2024 BYD Dolphin Mini**, by [tonielpro520](https://sketchfab.com/tonielpro520), [source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2024-byd-dolphin-mini-c4a97baaadda487faa7ed90ecd1b057d), Creative Commons Attribution. Downloaded from [autoford-carros3d](https://github.com/alexiskara/autoford-carros3d/blob/main/assets/byd-dolphin-mini.glb).
- **Chevrolet Onix 2024**, by [UZBEK 3D](https://sketchfab.com/Uzbek3D), [source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/chevrolet-onix-2024-bcc8987606db4088b039e55fe70fa379), Creative Commons Attribution. Downloaded from [autoford-carros3d](https://github.com/alexiskara/autoford-carros3d/blob/main/assets/chevrolet-onix.glb).

Bridge, city, environment, and animations are built procedurally in this project. The vehicles have been adapted for lighting, scale, and movement. The original sources identify the models and authors; model and manufacturer marks do not imply endorsement.
