## The charges (June 2025)

- U.S. Attorney's Office, Central District of California, release of 17 June 2025 (to fetch): seven men, indicted
  11 June 2025; "the largest jewelry heist in U.S. history"; "approximately $100 million worth of gold, diamonds,
  rubies, emeralds, and luxury watches".
- Names in the release: Carlos Victor Mestanza Cercado (31, Pasadena), Jazael Padilla Resto (36, in an Arizona
  prison), Pablo Raul Lugo Larroig (41, Rialto), Victor Hugo Valencia Solorzano (60), Jorge Enrique Alban (33),
  Jeson Nelon Presilla Flores (42, Upland), Eduardo Macias Ibarra (36).
- "Every defendant is presumed to be innocent until and unless proven guilty in court."
- Some jewelry recovered in searches on 16 June 2025; much still missing (AUSA Kevin Butler).
- Earlier thefts in the indictment: Samsung electronics ($240,573, March 2022, Ontario), Apple AirTags ($57,377;
  a knife and a threat), a crowbar at a Fontana rest stop, $14,081 of Samsung electronics (May 2022).
- Sources: Guardian 18 Jun 2025, NPR 18 Jun 2025 (14-page indictment), National Jeweler, CBS LA, Rapaport,
  Bloomberg (a 27-minute stop), Washington Examiner (about 2 a.m.; one guard went in to eat, the other asleep in
  the berth).

## The night, from the civil suits (2022)

- Brink's suit (Aug 2022): two armed guards; Driver 1 spent about 27-30 minutes getting food at the Flying J;
  Driver 2 asleep in the berth, saw and heard nothing; the red plastic seal and the rear lock were cut.
- Value: jewelers say about $100 million (suit sought $200 million); Brink's says the declared value was $8.7
  million and that caps what it owes. Unresolved as far as these excerpts go.
