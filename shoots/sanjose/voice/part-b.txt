Colombia says they were ten kilometres off. In nineteen eighty-four the finder's share was cut from half to five per cent. A Colombian heritage watchdog says the site was already disturbed, in twenty sixteen and twenty twenty two, and that the coordinates leaked. The state institute denies it. The government says this is science, not treasure. President Petro wants her raised before his term ends. So far: a cannon in a refrigerated container, coins kept in salt water, and the cups. The rest stays where it is, at the bottom, with the men who went down with her. So who is she: the country she sank off, the country that lost her, the nation whose silver she carries, or the men who say they found her first?
