In seventeen-oh-eight, a Spanish treasure ship blew up off Colombia, taking six hundred men and eleven million coins down with her. They call her the Holy Grail of shipwrecks. Estimates of what she carries run from four billion dollars to twenty. Colombia found her in twenty fifteen, at two thousand feet. Her coordinates are a state secret. Last November a robot lifted the first objects: a bronze cannon, three coins, and two porcelain cups. Which is the difficulty. The richest wreck in the world, and what came up first was the crockery. Spain says she is a Spanish warship, and a war grave. The Qhara Qhara of Bolivia say the silver came out of their mountain. An American salvage firm says it found her in nineteen eighty-two, and wants ten billion dollars: half of everything.
