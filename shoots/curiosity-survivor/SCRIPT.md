## Narration
He survived an atomic bomb. Then he went home to Nagasaki. In August nineteen forty-five, Tsutomu Yamaguchi was in Hiroshima on a business trip. When the bomb exploded, he suffered terrible burns. He survived the night, then travelled home by train. Home was Nagasaki. Three days after Hiroshima, a second atomic bomb struck that city. Yamaguchi survived again. He wasn't the only person to experience both bombings. But Japan later officially recognised him as a survivor of both. He lived to ninety-three, and spoke out against nuclear weapons. His story wasn't about being lucky. It was about making sure nobody had to survive the same horror again.
