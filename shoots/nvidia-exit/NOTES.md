- "Jen-Hsun Huang, 63, was born in Taiwan to a chemical engineer father and a schoolteacher mother."

  1992. A year later he founded Nvidia with fellow engineers Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem, during a meeting in a
  booth at Denny's. The name is a riff on the Latin term for envy, invidia".
- "Nvidia started out making graphics chips for personal computers. It released what is widely regarded as the world's
  first GPU, the GeForce 256, in 1999".
- "The company that Huang co-founded makes the computer chips that are integral to the development and operation of AI
  systems such as ChatGPT." "In technical jargon, its AI chips are known as graphics processor units (GPUs), which were
  originally designed to handle the graphics for video games."
- "Six months after the launch of ChatGPT, Nvidia's valuation reached $1tn (£756bn) in May 2023 and kept growing, hitting
  $5tn on October last year – the first ever company to reach such a milestone. However, it has fallen to about $4tn
  this week".
- "Huang's 3.6% stake in Nvidia has given him considerable wealth. His fortune is valued at $145bn, according to
  Bloomberg, the 13th highest in the world."
