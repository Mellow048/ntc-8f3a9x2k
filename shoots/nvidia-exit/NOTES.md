## C. How he started

**GUA-P** — The Guardian, Dan Milmo, "From washing dishes at Denny's to rockstar of AI: the rise and fall of Nvidia's

(<https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/12/rise-and-fall-of-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-profile>), part 1 of 4
read (it holds the biography):

- "Jensen Huang wasn't always in his trademark leather jacket. When the multibillionaire, now former Nvidia chief
  executive was a teenager in the US, his first job was waiting tables and washing dishes at a branch of Denny's. "It
  teaches you humility, it teaches you hard work, it teaches you hospitality," he said in 2023 of his time at the
  diner chain."
- "Jen-Hsun Huang, 63, was born in Taiwan to a chemical engineer father and a schoolteacher mother."
- "After a spell living in Thailand, Huang moved to the US when he was 10 – he is a joint citizen of Taiwan and the US –
  and has said he is "always an immigrant"."
- "Accidentally enrolled by his uncle into a juvenile reform school in Kentucky, he anglicised his name and showed
  educational flair. Huang went to high school in Oregon and in 1984 he graduated with a degree in electrical
  engineering from Oregon State University".
- "He then completed a master's degree in the same subject at Stanford University – in the heart of Silicon Valley – in
  1992. A year later he founded Nvidia with fellow engineers Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem, during a meeting in a
  booth at Denny's. The name is a riff on the Latin term for envy, invidia".
- "Nvidia started out making graphics chips for personal computers. It released what is widely regarded as the world's
  first GPU, the GeForce 256, in 1999".
- "The company that Huang co-founded makes the computer chips that are integral to the development and operation of AI
  systems such as ChatGPT." "In technical jargon, its AI chips are known as graphics processor units (GPUs), which were
  originally designed to handle the graphics for video games."
- "Six months after the launch of ChatGPT, Nvidia's valuation reached $1tn (£756bn) in May 2023 and kept growing, hitting
  $5tn on October last year – the first ever company to reach such a milestone. However, it has fallen to about $4tn
  this week".
- "Huang's 3.6% stake in Nvidia has given him considerable wealth. His fortune is valued at $145bn, according to
  Bloomberg, the 13th highest in the world."
