Then they went looking for the man who buried it. The previous owner of the house. He was dead. No heir came forward. In November the prefecture said the gold was not heritage, and it went to the man with the pool. Which leaves the tax. Finding it costs nothing. Selling it costs eleven and a half per cent, flat: eighty thousand five hundred euros. He keeps six hundred and nineteen thousand five hundred. He has refused every interview. The local paper calls him the man who got rich digging a pool. Somebody put seven hundred thousand euros in plastic bags in that garden, and never came back for it. So who was he: a man hiding his savings from a bank, from a divorce, from the tax office, or from whoever came next?
