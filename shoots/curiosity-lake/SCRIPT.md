## Fact boundaries
The drilling breach is the standard historical explanation; the flooded mine prevented definitive inspection of the precise initiating failure. Do not claim a proven surveying error. Eleven barges lost, nine resurfaced; no exact miner count.

## Narration
A drilling crew accidentally helped turn a lake into a giant drain. In nineteen eighty, an oil rig was drilling beneath Louisiana's Lake Peigneur. Under the lake was something else: a working salt mine. The drilling breached the mine, and water rushed underground. Think of pulling the plug in a bathtub. Except this drain kept getting bigger. A huge whirlpool swallowed the drilling rig and eleven barges. A canal even reversed direction, bringing water back toward the disappearing lake. Remarkably, the miners escaped, and nobody died. As the lake refilled, nine of those barges came back up. The mine did not.
