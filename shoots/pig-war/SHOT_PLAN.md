## Continuity notes

- Cutlar's cabin, the patch and Griffin's fence are one location, shot from
  four directions. The patch must be the same patch in shots 1, 2, 17–24.
- The pig appears alive in 1, 2, 10, 16, 17, 18; drawn on the ledger in 22;
  never shown dead in close-up.
- Uniforms: US infantry blue with the 1858 forage cap; Royal Marines red with
  white cross-belts; Royal Navy blue. Pickett's coat changes colour only in 45.
- Ships: five hulls, distinguishable by name board only in 29; never a
  generic block — hull, gunports, yards, rigging, boats.
- Nothing in frame after 0:52 carries a modern object; shots 57–60 are the
  only modern dress in the film.
