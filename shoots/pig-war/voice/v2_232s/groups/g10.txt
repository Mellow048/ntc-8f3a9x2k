Washington was horrified. President Buchanan sent General Winfield Scott, who talked both sides into a joint occupation: one company each, a hundred men, until the diplomats settled it. Harney was recalled. The diplomats took twelve years. The marines built English Camp on Garrison Bay: barracks, a hospital, a blockhouse, tennis courts, a bowling green, a library, a billiard room, and a garden. On the hill above, a small cemetery. The Americans held the windy south end, and waited.
