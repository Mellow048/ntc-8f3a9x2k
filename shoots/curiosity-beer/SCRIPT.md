## Narration
London once suffered a flood made entirely of beer. On October seventeenth, eighteen fourteen, a giant wooden vat stood inside the Horse Shoe Brewery. Iron hoops held it together. When one failed, the vat burst. Beer smashed through the brewery wall and poured into the streets of Saint Giles. But this wasn't free drinks. It was a disaster. Homes were damaged. Cellars flooded. Eight people died. Neighbours searched the wreckage, keeping quiet so they could hear anyone trapped. And afterward? The brewery received tax relief on the beer it had lost.
